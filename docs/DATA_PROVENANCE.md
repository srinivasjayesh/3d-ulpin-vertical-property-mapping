# Data Provenance

Hyderabad uses OSM, the Google Open Buildings temporal 2.5D raster (2023 tile) and Telangana TGRAC BhuNaksha. Manhattan uses NYC PLUTO, DoITT/OTI footprints and tax-lot data. Mumbai uses the MCGM G/South ward layer, a BMC building-footprint reference layer and an OSM snapshot.

Hyderabad parcel confidence is geometric: coverage 40%, parcel/building ratio 20%, sharing 20% and interpretation 20%, followed by caps for site/multi-building cases. Labels are estimates, not probabilities.

Hero records B0595, B0612 and B0589 are ordinary Hyderabad rows selected for demonstration. B0595 is a plot-like 97.9 estimate; B0612 and B0589 are site-like capped 88 estimates. No owner, title or landmark name is inferred.

Source URLs and retrieval dates are retained in profile artifacts and pipeline scripts. Raw attributes are preserved where possible.
