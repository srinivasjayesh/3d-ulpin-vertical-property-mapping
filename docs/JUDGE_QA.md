# Judge Q&A

**What is ULPIN here?** A prototype vertical identifier; not an official government ULPIN.

**What is real?** Source footprints, published service layers and retained source attributes. Floors and units are models unless explicitly sourced.

**How are floors made?** Height divided by floor count; equal-height strata.

**Are units surveyed?** No. Manhattan units are deterministic footprint strips; Mumbai has none.

**Why are basements limited?** Current sources do not provide count, depth or geometry; B1/B2 is a labelled demonstration.

**What does confidence mean?** A geometric Hyderabad parcel-match score, not probability or title accuracy.

**Why Three.js?** To inspect vertical relationships interactively; geometry is approximate.

**Why PostGIS?** Optional scalable spatial storage and indexed queries; file mode remains the default.

**What is the business buyer?** Municipal GIS/property-tax/planning teams, often with an integrator.

**Who competes?** Genesys, Esri/integrators, property-survey specialists and internal GIS teams.

**Why pay?** A governed building-to-floor evidence trail can reduce manual reconciliation; ROI must be proven in a pilot.

**What are realistic margins?** Planning gross margins are 25–40% projects and 60–75% recurring, not guaranteed net margin.

**What is TAM?** A transparent 500-city proxy at ₹20–40 lakh annual = ₹100–200 crore/year; it is not the entire GIS market.

**What is first SOM?** 2–4 ward pilots, ₹42 lakh–₹1.88 crore aggregate scenario over 18–24 months.

**Can it determine ownership or violations?** No; those require authoritative records and legal processes.
